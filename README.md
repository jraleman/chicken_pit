### Possible Issues

- The game may not work properly on Safari due to WebGL limitations.
- Audio may not play on iOS devices due to restrictions on autoplaying audio.
- Multiplayer mode may have issues with synchronization between players.
- Some audio effects may not trigger correctly in certain scenarios.
- The game may not scale properly on very small screens.
- The game may experience performance issues on low-end devices.
- The game may not work properly in older browsers that do not support WebGL or modern JavaScript features.
